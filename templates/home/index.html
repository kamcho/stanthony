What We Offer

Our Medical Services

A faith-based facility offering out-patient, in-patient, maternity, laboratory, X-ray, ultra-sound, MCH, PMTCT, PITC/HTS, CCC and referral services.

Out-patient Services

Day care without overnight admission.

  • General consultations
  • Minor procedures & dressings
  • Chronic illness follow-up
  • Prescription & pharmacy support

In-patient Care

Admission with continuous nursing care.

  • Ward admission & observation
  • 24/7 nursing support
  • Treatment & monitoring
  • Discharge & aftercare guidance

Maternity & MCH

Care for mothers, newborns and children.

  • Antenatal care (ANC)
  • Delivery & postnatal care
  • Immunisation & growth monitoring
  • Family planning support

Laboratory Investigations

Basic tests plus biochemistry.

  • Kidney function tests
  • Liver function tests
  • Cholesterol, sodium & potassium
  • Routine blood & urine tests

X-ray & Ultra-sound

Diagnostic imaging for clinical decisions.

  • Chest & limb X-ray
  • Pelvic X-ray
  • Obstetric ultra-sound (US)
  • Abdominal & pelvic US

CCC for HIV/AIDS

Comprehensive care with continuity and dignity.

  • HIV clinical follow-up
  • ART adherence support
  • CD4 / viral load linkage
  • Counselling & psychosocial care

PMTCT & PITC/HTS

HIV prevention and testing services.

  • PMTCT for expectant mothers
  • Provider-initiated testing (PITC)
  • HIV testing services (HTS)
  • Partner & family testing support

Referral & Morgue

Support beyond primary facility care.

  • Specialist referral coordination
  • Ambulance / transfer support
  • Morgue services
  • Family guidance & documentation
Who We Are

Serving Abossi
Since 1983

St. Anthony's Health Centre Abossi is a faith-based organisation offering out-patient, in-patient, maternity, laboratory investigations (including biochemistry), X-ray, ultra-sound, maternal and child health, PMTCT, PITC/HTS, CCC for persons living with HIV/AIDS, and referral services.

Established in 1983 at Abossi Village and managed by the Little Sisters of St. Joseph under the Catholic Diocese of Ngong, our 29 medical and support staff serve Trans Mara East, Narok County—open 24/7.

  • 29 Dedicated Staff Medical and support professionals serving the community
  • Faith-Based Stewardship Catholic Diocese of Ngong · Little Sisters of St. Joseph
  • Accessible by All Quality, safe and affordable healthcare for every family
