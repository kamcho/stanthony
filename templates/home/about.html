Hospital History & Background

St. Anthony's Health Centre Abossi was established in 1983 at Abossi Village, Trans Mara East, Narok County, to improve the health and living standards of the community of Abossi through provision of quality, safe and affordable health care services accessible by all.

The facility operates under the stewardship of His Lordship Rt. Rev. Bishop John Oballa Owaa of the Catholic Diocese of Ngong and is managed by the Little Sisters of St. Joseph, with direction from the father in charge, Holy Trinity Catholic Church, Abossi.

"Transforming lives through evangelization, pastoral care and health-care service provision that is affordable, reliable and accessible by all." — Our Mission
